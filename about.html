About Your Sitter

Hi, we’re the Karis.

Kare By Kari didn’t begin as a business. It grew from our family’s love for dogs. With our passion for travel and our kids’ busy schedules, we weren’t ready to commit to one dog of our own. Instead, we began fostering dogs during the holidays and volunteering with the SPCA whenever we could.

Over time, fostering and volunteering naturally led to dog sitting and walking for neighbors and friends. As caring for family dogs left us with less time to foster, we found another way to keep supporting animals in need.

At the end of each year, the Karis match our dog-sitting earnings and donate the matched amount to the SPCA. Every stay gives your dog loving, family-style care while helping another animal find a better life.
