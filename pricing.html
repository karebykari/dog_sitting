Services & Pricing

Simple pricing, no surprise fees.

Simply drop your dog off at our home for any service. All care takes place here—we do not travel to customers’ homes. Food, water, and playtime are included as needed.

Overnight Boarding

$35 / night

Your dog stays at our home with full run of the house, not a crate or kennel run.

  • Two walks a day
  • Meals on your dog's schedule
  • Photo update each evening
  • Free meet & greet before first stay

Day Stay

$25 / stay

Drop your dog off at our home for a daytime stay while you’re busy or on the go.

  • Feeding & fresh water
  • Potty break in our yard or on-leash
  • Quick play or cuddle time
  • Photo & text update after each visit

Dog Walking

$20 / walk

  • Solo walks — no unfamiliar dogs mixed in
  • Route matched to your dog's fitness level
  • Fresh water bowl refill on return

Holidays & peak dates

A $10/night holiday rate applies for major holidays, since these dates book up fastest — check availability early.
